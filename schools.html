What we offer schools

🎤 Invite-a-scientist talks

Online or in-person talks by researchers — from AI and space to medicine and materials — in Kazakh, Russian or English, matched to your students' age group.

🔬 Research mentoring

Guidance for student research projects, science fairs and olympiad preparation from researchers working in the field.

🧭 Career guidance

How to choose a field, apply abroad, win scholarships — answered by people who did it, from Astana to Boston to Tokyo.

How it works

Tell us your school, topic of interest and preferred format. We match you with a volunteering member of the community and coordinate the session — at no cost to the school.
